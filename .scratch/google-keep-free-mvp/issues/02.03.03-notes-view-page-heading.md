# 02.03.03: Notes view page heading

**Status:** in-progress
**Lane:** Quick (one heading and one assertion)
**Parent:** ✅02.03-composer-and-notes-grid.md
**Blocked by:** None
**Unblocks:** None

## Purpose

Give the Notes home page the `h1` the design calls for, so the cards' `h2` headings have a parent and axe's `page-has-heading-one` stops flagging `/`. Filed from the 02.03.01 review (finding N3): tickets 06 and 11 give the Archive and label views a heading, and nothing gave the Notes view one.

## Scope

- A visible or visually hidden `h1` on `/` (ui-spec section 19: page/view heading `text-lg font-semibold`). Settle at scope-check whether it reads "Notes" and whether it shows on screen.
- A check in the signed-in shell E2E that the page has one `h1`.

## Out of scope

The Archive and label headings (tickets 06 and 11), any change to the shell layout, the composer (02.03.02).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `/` has exactly one `h1`, and the cards' `h2` headings sit under it.
- [ ] An axe-core scan of `/` in light and dark reports no `page-has-heading-one` violation.
- [ ] The heading looks right in light and dark and does not shift the grid or the empty state.

## Testing expectations

An E2E assertion in `e2e/shell.spec.ts` for one `h1`, and an axe-core scan run locally as in 02.03.01's quality check.

## Decisions

- decided by user: the heading is a visible `h1` reading "Notes", styled `text-lg font-semibold` (ui-spec section 19). It is not visually hidden. This supersedes the "Notes is implicit" line in ui-spec section 4, which is updated to match.
- decided by user: the release is `v0.2.33` (newest tag is `v0.2.32`), in a separate `chore(release): v0.2.33` commit.
- decided by agent: the `h1` goes in `app/page.tsx` above the Composer, as `px-4 pt-4`, so the Composer and the grid keep their own spacing. Rejected: putting it inside the Composer (it is a view heading, not part of the composer) and inside `NoteGrid` (it must also show over the empty state).
