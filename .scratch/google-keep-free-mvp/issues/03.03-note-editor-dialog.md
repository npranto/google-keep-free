## Purpose

An Owner can click a Note, edit its title and body in a dialog with no Save button, see
"Saving…" then "Saved", and find the edit still there after a reload.

## Out of scope

The version-conflict banner (04), the pin, color, label, archive and trash toolbar buttons, the
read-only variant for a Trashed Note, a full-screen layout on mobile (14).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clicking a Note card opens the dialog with its current title and body, including a body
      longer than the card preview: 03.03.01 (the read), 03.03.02 (the dialog)
- [ ] Typing stops for 1s, the status shows "Saving…" then "Saved", and "Saved" fades after about
      2s: 03.03.02
- [ ] Blurring a field, closing the dialog, or hiding the tab flushes immediately: 03.03.02
- [ ] Closing and reopening, or refreshing, shows the saved content, and the grid card shows it
      too: 03.03.02
- [ ] The dialog traps focus, closes on Escape, and returns focus to the card: 03.03.02

## Demo instructions

Open a Note, edit the title and body, watch the status region, stop typing, confirm "Saved",
refresh, and the edit persisted.
