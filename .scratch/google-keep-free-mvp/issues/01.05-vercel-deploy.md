# 01.05: Vercel deploy of the foundation

**Status:** in-progress
**Parent:** 01-application-foundation.md
**Blocked by:** 01.04
**Unblocks:** 02

## Purpose

Confirm the foundation works in the real hosting environment.

## Scope

Vercel project link, env vars configured (Neon, Clerk), preview deploy.

## Out of scope

Custom domain, extra CI.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deployed preview redirects signed-out visitors to Clerk sign-in.
- [x] After sign-in the deployed app shows the empty shell.

## Demo / verification

Visit the preview URL signed-out, sign in, see the shell.

## Review budget / risk

Tiny. Human dashboard steps (candidate for the wizard skill). Risk: env mismatch
between local and Vercel.

## Comments
