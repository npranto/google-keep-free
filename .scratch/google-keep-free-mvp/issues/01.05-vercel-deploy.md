# 01.05: Vercel deploy of the foundation

**Status:** ready-for-agent
**Parent:** 01-application-foundation.md
**Blocked by:** 01.04
**Unblocks:** 02

## Purpose

Confirm the foundation works in the real hosting environment.

## Scope

Vercel project link, env vars configured (Neon, Clerk), preview deploy.

## Out of scope

Custom domain, extra CI.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deployed preview redirects signed-out visitors to Clerk sign-in.
- [ ] After sign-in the deployed app shows the empty shell.

## Demo / verification

Visit the preview URL signed-out, sign in, see the shell.

## Review budget / risk

Tiny. Human dashboard steps (candidate for the wizard skill). Risk: env mismatch
between local and Vercel.

## Comments

**Assumptions changed by 01.03** (PR #25, shipped Fri 9 Oct):
- The Vercel project is already linked, auto-deploys `main` to production, and has
  `DATABASE_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLERK_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` and `CLERK_SECRET_KEY` set for
  Production and Preview. Check those off rather than redoing them.
- "Custom domain" is no longer out of scope: a live Clerk instance cannot use
  `*.vercel.app`, so production is served at https://gfk.lol (A record `216.198.79.1`,
  `www` CNAME to `cname.vercel-dns.com`) with Clerk's DNS records and Google OAuth.
- Production already redirects signed-out visitors to Clerk sign-in. What is left is
  the second criterion (the empty shell) once 01.04 ships, plus the redirect from
  `google-keep-free.vercel.app` to `gfk.lol`.
- Clerk shows "Communication locked" on the production instance, so email codes do not
  work until Clerk support unlocks it.
