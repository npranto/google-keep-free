## Purpose

An Owner can click "Take a note...", write a Note, close the composer, and see the Note in
the grid, including after a full refresh.

## Out of scope

Editing a Note, autosave, pin, color, labels, archive, trash, toolbar icons, `loading.tsx`,
`error.tsx`, a visual limit counter (the server enforces limits; the composer reuses the
constants from `lib/notes/validation.ts` for `maxLength` only).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Typing into the composer and closing it with content creates an Active Note that
      appears in the grid: 02.03.02
- [ ] Closing an empty composer performs no database write and shows no new card: 02.03.02
- [ ] After a full page refresh, the created Note is still in the grid: 02.03.02
- [ ] The composer, grid and cards look right on desktop width, in light and dark:
      02.03.01 (grid and cards), 02.03.02 (composer)

## Demo instructions

Sign in, click "Take a note...", type a title and body, click elsewhere to close, refresh
the page, and the Note still shows.
